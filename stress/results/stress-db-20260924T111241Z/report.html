- Atomicity: a multi-statement transaction that throws must leave no partial stock or stock_log change.

- Consistency: unique email/barcode constraints and conditional stock updates must reject invalid states.

- Isolation: concurrent decrements on one row must leave stock equal to initial minus successful updates.

- Durability: a committed insert must be readable afterward.
